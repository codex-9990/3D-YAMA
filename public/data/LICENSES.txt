3D-YAMA: public eastern Suma Alps sample data

These data licenses are separate from the application code license.

terrain.json, source-dem5a-15-*.png
Source: Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI) DEM5A PNG elevation tiles.
Attribution: 地理院タイル（標高タイル）を加工して作成
Every decoded native zoom-15 elevation sample in the selected rectangle is
preserved. The original DEM source has nominal 5 m resolution. The roughly
3.93 m map-tile pixel spacing does NOT imply a finer ground survey. Four source
PNG tiles are retained so the offline verifier can check every output height.
No extra elevation noise, upsampling, zero-filling, or invented pinnacles.
Public Data License 1.0 (PDL 1.0), under the GSI content terms:
https://www.gsi.go.jp/kikakuchousei/kikakuchousei40182.html
https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/ichiran.html#dem
https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/demtile.html

The OSM-derived data remains under ODbL 1.0. Preserve its attribution and license
when reusing or distributing it. Complete machine-readable source geometry for
all used trail ways, woodland and OSM points is included in sample-osm-source.geojson.
The forest source tag references MLIT Japan's 2006 woodland-area data. It is a
land-use envelope, not an individual-tree map or proof of present vegetation.
GPX and scene placement elevations are approximate bilinear samples of the GSI
ground grid; GSI source and processing attribution also apply.

Photograph rights remain with their respective owners. No permission to reuse
these tourism photographs, logos, or maps is inferred. Only links and original
factual descriptions are included; no photo textures or copied prose.

The files contain only public map data. The route is a generated demonstration
of connected mapped trails, not a recorded walk or a route recommendation.
There are no personal photographs, activity times, user notes or private tracks.
Trees, small rock detail, tread count, widths, risers and handrails are visual
illustration, not surveyed or field-verified. The 400-step alignment is mapped;
the short western 馬の背 staircase is photo-correlated approximate placement,
not a mapped highway=steps way. Both are expressly distinguished in metadata.
Current access, closures, safety, survey dates and field accuracy are unverified.
Do not use the sample for navigation or safety decisions.
See provenance.json for transformations, source IDs, hashes and validation.
