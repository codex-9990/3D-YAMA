The OSM-derived data remains under ODbL 1.0. Preserve its attribution and license
when reusing or distributing it. Machine-readable source geometry for all used
trail edges and peaks is included as sample-osm-source.geojson.
GPX elevation and peak placement elevations are approximate bilinear samples of
the GSI terrain grid; GSI source and processing attribution also apply.

The files contain only public map data. The route is a generated demonstration
of connected mapped trails, not a recorded walk or a route recommendation.
There are no personal photographs, activity times, user notes or private tracks.
Current access, closures, safety, survey dates and field accuracy are unverified.
Do not use the sample for navigation or safety decisions.
See provenance.json for transformation details, source IDs, hashes and checks.
